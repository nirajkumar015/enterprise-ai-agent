SHIPPING POLICY

Standard delivery generally takes 3-7 business days after an order has been shipped.

Order Processing:
Orders are normally processed within 1-2 business days.

Delivery Delays:
Delivery times may be affected by weather, holidays, incorrect addresses, logistics problems, or other unexpected circumstances.

Tracking:
Customers can use the tracking information provided after the order has been shipped.

Delayed Orders:
If an order has not arrived within the expected delivery period, customers should contact support with their order information.

Address Changes:
Customers should contact support as soon as possible if they need to correct a shipping address. Address changes may not be possible after an order has been shipped.

International Shipping:
International delivery times may vary depending on customs and local delivery services.
