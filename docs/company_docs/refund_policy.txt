REFUND POLICY

Our company accepts refund requests for eligible products within 30 days of delivery.

Eligibility:
- The product must have been purchased through an approved sales channel.
- The refund request must be submitted within 30 days of delivery.
- The product should be returned in its original condition whenever possible.
- Proof of purchase may be required.

Damaged Products:
If a product arrives damaged, the customer should contact support as soon as possible and provide photographs or other evidence of the damage.

Refund Processing:
Once the returned product is inspected and the refund is approved, the refund is generally processed within 5-7 business days.

Non-Refundable Cases:
Products that are clearly damaged due to customer misuse may not qualify for a refund.

For questions about refund eligibility, customers should contact the support team.
