FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: How long does standard shipping take?
A: Standard delivery generally takes 3-7 business days after the order has been shipped.

Q: How long do I have to request a refund?
A: Eligible refund requests should normally be submitted within 30 days of delivery.

Q: How long is the standard warranty?
A: Eligible products have a standard warranty period of 12 months from the date of purchase.

Q: What should I do if my product arrives damaged?
A: Contact support as soon as possible and provide photographs or other evidence of the damage.

Q: What should I do if my laptop does not turn on?
A: Check the power adapter, charging indicator, power outlet, and external devices. If the issue continues, contact technical support.

Q: What should I do if my order is delayed?
A: Check the tracking information first. If the order is outside the expected delivery period, contact support with your order information.

Q: Can I change my shipping address?
A: Contact support as soon as possible. Address changes may not be possible after the order has been shipped.

Q: How can I contact support?
A: Customers should contact the company's support team for assistance with orders, refunds, warranty claims, and technical problems.
