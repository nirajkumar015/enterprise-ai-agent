WARRANTY POLICY

Eligible products are covered by a limited warranty against manufacturing defects.

Warranty Period:
The standard warranty period is 12 months from the date of purchase.

Covered Issues:
- Manufacturing defects
- Hardware failures caused by manufacturing defects
- Components that stop functioning under normal use

Not Covered:
- Accidental physical damage
- Damage caused by liquid exposure
- Unauthorized modifications
- Normal cosmetic wear
- Damage caused by improper use

Warranty Claims:
Customers should provide proof of purchase and a description of the issue.

If the issue is covered by warranty, the company may repair or replace the affected product depending on the circumstances.

For warranty assistance, customers should contact the support team.
